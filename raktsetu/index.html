How RaktSetu finds a donor

Four steps stand between an emergency and a matched, willing donor.

1

Donors register once

Blood group, age, city and contact details are saved to a live donor directory.

2

A request goes out

A patient, attendee or hospital posts the blood group needed, units, and location.

3

Compatible donors surface

RaktSetu matches compatible blood groups and ranks donors by distance and availability.

4

Donors respond, life is saved

Nearby donors are alerted instantly and can confirm directly with the hospital or patient.

Know who can donate to whom

RaktSetu uses standard blood-group compatibility rules to match every request with the right donors automatically — no guesswork for patients or attendees.

  • 🩸 O‑ negative is the universal donor — compatible with all eight groups.
  • 🩸 AB+ positive is the universal recipient — can receive from any group.
  • 🩸 Matching is applied automatically the moment a request is posted.
