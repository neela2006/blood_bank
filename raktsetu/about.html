About RaktSetu

RaktSetu — meaning "blood bridge" — is a MERN-stack platform built to connect blood donors with patients and hospitals in real time, replacing slow, manual donor searches with a live, searchable directory and instant emergency alerts.

Motivation

Blood shortages during emergencies often cost lives, and today's manual search — phone calls, social media posts, word of mouth — is simply too slow. There is no centralized way to know, instantly, who is available to donate.

  • 🚨 Sends real-time emergency alerts to nearby donors
  • 📍 Locates the nearest available donors and blood banks
  • 🗂️ Maintains donor and blood-stock records digitally

Need, relevance & significance

Need: reduce delay in finding donors, prevent shortages during emergencies, and support hospitals and blood banks with reliable digital records.

Technical significance: a mobile/web application with a real-time notification system and a database for donor and stock records.

Practical significance: easy donor registration, quick emergency-request handling, and location-based donor search for patients, donors, hospitals, and blood banks alike.

Background study & research gap

Existing options — government portals, social media donor groups, and local blood-bank registers — offer some awareness, but leave a clear gap that RaktSetu is built to close.

Today, without RaktSetu

  • No live tracking of donor availability
  • Delayed response during emergencies
  • No centralized blood-stock database
  • Donors are not notified proactively

With RaktSetu

  • Donors register with blood group & location
  • Instant emergency alerts to nearby donors
  • Nearest available donor / blood bank located automatically
  • Live dashboard of blood stock & requests

Innovation: geo-located emergency alerts that connect donors and patients within minutes.

Limitations

✍️

Manual registration

Donors must register themselves; the system cannot auto-detect eligible people.

📶

Requires internet

Both donors and requesters need an internet connection to use the platform.

🔕

Device-dependent alerts

Alerts rely on each donor's device notification settings.
